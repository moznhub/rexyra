# Code of Conduct

Rexyra follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Its full text applies to everyone taking part in this project: in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space.

In short:

- Be respectful and considerate. Disagree about the work, not about the person.
- Assume good intent, and give and accept feedback gracefully.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination or unwelcome personal attention of any kind.
- Respect people's time and privacy.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behaviour that goes against this code, contact the maintainers privately through the [moznhub](https://github.com/moznhub) organisation on GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially, and nobody will be penalised for making one in good faith.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code, and may ban, temporarily or permanently, anyone who repeatedly or seriously breaks it.
